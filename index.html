A little organized chaos.

0 steps

Langton's ant: on white, turn right; on black, turn left. Flip the square and move forward. A two-dimensional Turing machine with simple rules and complex behavior.

What's happening?

Two rules turn a blank grid into a tangled trail, then a repeating “highway” after about 10,000 steps. Here, the ant wraps around when it reaches an edge.
